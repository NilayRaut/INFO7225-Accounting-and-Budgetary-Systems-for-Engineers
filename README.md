# INFO7225-Accounting-and-Budgetary-Systems-for-Engineers


This repository contains coursework, textbook chapters, and lecture-based materials for **INFO7225** at Northeastern University, taught by **Professor Shaoming Shi**. The focus of this course is on the integration of **financial accounting principles**, **managerial accounting techniques**, and the role of **accounting information systems (AIS)** within engineering and business processes.

## 🧠 Key Learning Objectives

- Understand **GAAP principles** and their application to business accounting.
- Distinguish between **financial and managerial accounting** purposes.
- Analyze **business transactions** using accounting systems.
- Apply **costing methods** (e.g., FIFO, LIFO, ABC, standard costing).
- Develop and interpret **budgets**, **variance reports**, and **financial statements**.
- Utilize **AIS** to support business process decision-making.
